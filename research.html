Selected projects

Research experience

01

MSc thesis research · August 2024–August 2026

Head and neck cancer recurrence classification

Machine Learning for Head and Neck Cancer Recurrence Classification Using Longitudinal Follow-up CT Imaging and Clinical Data

At the Faculty of Medicine, Ramathibodi Hospital, I constructed a longitudinal cohort of 791 patients and 2,694 post-treatment CT examinations, linking imaging with clinical information and recurrence evidence from hospital records. I developed and evaluated 3D CNN, CNN–GRU, and image–clinical models using PyTorch and MONAI, with patient-level data splitting and model evaluation.

Related output: First-author paper for presentation at NICGR2026. Publication details.

02

Research Assistant · September 2024–present

Nationwide healthcare claims in Thailand

At the Department of Clinical Epidemiology and Biostatistics, Mahidol University / Ramathibodi Hospital, I work with nationwide healthcare claims data from Thailand’s National Health Security Office on alcohol-related liver and gastrointestinal disease outcomes.

The work includes SQL data queries, Python-based preparation and analysis, Tableau map visualizations of Thailand-wide provincial data, cohort construction, survival analysis, confounding-variable analysis, and univariate and multivariate analyses. I collaborate with clinicians and epidemiologists on interpretation and manuscript preparation.

Related output: Manuscript under review. Publication details.

03

Research project · September 2025–present

LLM-assisted evidence extraction and study selection

At Mahidol University, I work on large-language-model-assisted workflows for structured evidence extraction and study selection in systematic reviews and meta-analyses. My contribution includes data curation, formal analysis, software, and writing/reviewing.

I have worked with MedGemma through LM Studio and Gemini. The published study compares open-source and closed-source models; its evaluated models include Gemini 2.0 Flash, Llama 3.1, and Qwen 2.5.

Related output: Published in PLOS Digital Health (2026).

04

Data Science Fellow · April–June 2024

LLM confidence calibration

At Fellowship.AI, I investigated whether language-model confidence estimates reflected prediction correctness, with an emphasis on reliability and uncertainty in high-stakes AI systems. The evaluation used the MMLU dataset, with larger models such as Gemini and Llama judging smaller models including Mistral and Stable Beluga.
